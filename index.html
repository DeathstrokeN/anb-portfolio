About

Research-driven engineering with strong applied focus.

I work at the intersection of robotics, advanced control, optimization, and AI-assisted perception. My current PhD research focuses on improving the accuracy of cable-driven parallel robots through predictive control, stiffness-aware modeling, and realistic simulation frameworks.

What I build
